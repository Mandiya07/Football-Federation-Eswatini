## 1. Membership & Subscription Tiers

### 1. Institutional Club Membership (Annual)
- **Eligibility**: Football clubs registered in Eswatini meeting Article I criteria.
- **Entitlements**: Full Federation voting rights, League participation eligibility, Academy talent progression pipeline access.
- **Cost**: Mandated annual licensing levy.

### 2. Fan & Supporter Digital Pass (Future Commercial Phase)
- **Eligibility**: General public and football supporters worldwide.
- **Entitlements**: HD Live match streaming, archive access, exclusive club content, matchday ticketing priority.
- **Governance**: Supporters Unions may organize independently but do NOT hold Federation voting membership (Article I).

### 3. Investor Prospectus Access Tier
- **Eligibility**: Vetted commercial partners and infrastructure developers.
- **Entitlements**: Access to stadium infrastructure briefs, commercial investment dossiers, and sponsorship opportunities (`/investment`).
