## 1. Commercial Architecture & Revenue Streams

The commercial model of the Football Federation Eswatini and the Indvundvundvwane Major Football League operates outside of FIFA dependency, adhering to complete institutional and financial self-reliance.

### Core Revenue Streams:
1. **Corporate League Title & Secondary Sponsorships**: Commercial partnerships supporting the 16-team top flight and dedicated Women's Football League.
2. **Broadcasting & Streaming Rights**: Direct-to-consumer and syndicated distribution rights for league fixtures.
3. **Club Franchise & Annual Licensing Levies**: Standardized administrative fees paid by participating clubs to fund independent match officiating, anti-corruption monitoring, and executive staff (League Executive, Accountant, Secretary, Messenger).
4. **Investor Capital Allocation (Private Placements)**: Institutional equity and infrastructure funding managed under the Investment portal (`/investment`).

---

## 2. Financial Governance & Anti-Corruption Rules

1. **Dual-Signoff Treasury**: All federation and league company disbursements require co-authorization by the Board-elected League Executive and Chief Financial Officer.
2. **Independent Audit Mandate**: Financial statements are audited annually by an accredited external accounting firm appointed by the 16 club shareholders.
3. **No Executive Enrichment**: Relatives and close affiliates of Federation executives are strictly prohibited from receiving procurement contracts or commercial concessions (Article III compliance).
