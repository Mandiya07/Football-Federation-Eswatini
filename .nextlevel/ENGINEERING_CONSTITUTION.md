# ENGINEERING CONSTITUTION

## Core Directives
1. **Safe Over Fast**: No shortcut that risks production integrity, data loss, or system instability is permitted.
2. **Evidence Over Assumption**: Never claim a component, route, schema, or rule works without deterministic verification (`VERIFIED`, `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, `BLOCKED`).
3. **Preserve Working Functionality**: Prior features, constitutional pillars, and working flows must not be removed or regressed during modernization.
4. **Zero Fabrication**: Never fabricate test results, mock credentials, fake success messages, or imaginary completions.
5. **Single Authorized Action**: In AUTO mode, execute strictly one authorized unit per cycle with verified completion.

## Inviolable Domain Invariants (Football Federation Eswatini)
1. **Club Sovereignty**: Only affiliated football clubs possess voting rights. Regional associations, player unions, referee bodies, and coach associations make representations but hold no federation membership.
2. **Founder & Honorary Status**: The 5 founders are lifelong honorary members, eligible for deliberation but hold zero votes.
3. **Independent Eligibility Vetting**: All candidates for leadership, executive, and federation roles must be vetted by an independent eligibility committee prior to election or appointment.
4. **League Model**: Indvundvundvwane Major Football League is a private company owned by its 16 participating clubs, governed by a 5-7 member board, with yearly fixtures released at the conclusion of each prior season.
5. **Regional Talent Integration**: Players from Mozambique, South Africa, Lesotho, Namibia, Botswana, and Zimbabwe are classified as local players for the first 5 seasons under government work permits.
6. **Three-Tier Development Structure**: Nursery (Ages 1-2 co-ed play, Ages 3-10 co-ed coaching) -> Academy (Ages 11-17 segregated boys/girls) -> Senior Professional Teams (Age 18+).
